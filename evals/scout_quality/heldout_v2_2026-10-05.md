# Held-out Scout evaluation — 5 October 2026

## Method

Two held-out cases were rerun sequentially with the current request contract,
`OfflineDemoClient`, and frozen,
synthetic source fixtures. No live provider or model calls were made. The
independent review sheets hide Critic scores, rankings, and proposal outcomes.
The deterministic run and its synthetic inputs support contract/count checks;
they do not establish live Scout quality.

| Case | Requested | Returned | Mechanical outcome | Human review |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `mathematics-optimization-01` | 12 (18 internal target) | 5 | Unique candidate IDs and evidence references validated; `underproduction` recorded. | Five candidate rows prepared; ratings pending. |
| `insufficient-coverage-01` | 12 (18 internal target) | 0 | `source_unavailable` recorded; no unsupported candidate was produced. | No candidate rows to rate. |

The second case uses an explicit `allow_other_domain` review configuration so
the held-out marine-biology request can run against its frozen fixture. It
returned no sources, matching the case's insufficient-coverage setup.

## Pending human review

Complete every blank score and provide a reviewer ID in
`evals/reports/v2-06-current-mathematics-review.csv`. Score each dimension from
1 (poor) to 5 (excellent): request fit, constraint compliance, distinctiveness,
and support from the cited evidence. Add brief notes for any score of 1 or 5.
Judge only the candidate and its cited receipts against the request in that
row. Do not use proposal or Critic outcomes. This review is necessary before
V2-06 can be marked complete.

After the sheet is complete, record whether the `underproduction` reason is
accurate and run:

```sh
python -m evals.scout_quality score \
  --result evals/reports/v2-06-09-math.json \
  --case-id mathematics-optimization-01 \
  --review evals/reports/v2-06-current-mathematics-review.csv \
  --output evals/reports/v2-06-current-mathematics-summary.json \
  --shortfall-reason-accurate yes
```

The evaluator validates candidate count, ID uniqueness, evidence references,
diagnostic count consistency, shortfall presence, and the completed human
scores. The refreshed mechanical audits are
`evals/reports/v2-06-current-math-audit.json` and
`evals/reports/v2-06-current-insufficient-audit-v2.json`.

## Limits

This is two held-out synthetic cases and one candidate-level human review batch;
it is not a general quality or reliability estimate. It measures contract and
review workflow behavior, not live source relevance.
